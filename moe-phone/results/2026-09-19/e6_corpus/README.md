# E6 corpus (research spec E6), assembled 2026-09-19, before any E6 row
- `kl_text.txt`: the teacher-forced KL text. It concatenates the first 3,200 characters of two English Wikipedia articles
  (Photosynthesis, French Revolution; the MediaWiki extracts API), lines 1-140 of CPython 3.12's `Lib/heapq.py` (PSF licence), and
  one English user/assistant turn from OpenAssistant oasst1 (validation split, message 7d05acb7…, Apache-2.0). All of it is
  human-written, none of it generated by the model under test. It is ~13 kB, roughly 3,000 tokens, which also gives a long-context
  check (ESTIMAND §5).
- `mmlu/q000-q099.txt`: 100 MMLU test questions (cais/mmlu `all` test split, rows 0, 140, …, 13860; 51 subjects; answers A22 B27 C22 D29),
  zero-shot, "Answer:" format, scored by the first-token log-prob of " A"/" B"/" C"/" D" (`--ppl-choices`). Ground truth is in
  `mmlu_answers.json`; `mmlu100.json` holds the source rows.
