**Status: paused on 2026-09-18 at the phone session's request.** The user wants the work out of the
single-lever testing loop. The next phone comparison is one stacked configuration (SLRU + 0012 + the
best 0013 gate) against the baseline, whose effect should be large enough to resolve without new

Applies to BigMoeOnEdge @ 74ba18f **after 0001–0013** (engine files and `tests/config_test.cpp`;
`git apply --check` clean on the 0013 tree).

## Why

Between separate runs on the 15R the clock governor alone gives a 5.5% run-to-run standard deviation
(`bmoe_order3.json`, six identical rows). With 3 runs per arm that resolves only about 10% effects,
while every lever tested has been 3–9% (`research/2026-09-18_why_levers_flip.md`). Alternating the two
arms every N decode tokens inside one process gives both arms the same cache, clocks and thermal
state, and turns the block of N tokens into the unit of replication.

## What it does

- `--ab-toggle N` (0 = off): the arm flips every N decode tokens, starting with arm A, the lever as
  configured. Arm B is the lever off.
- `--ab-lever`:
  - `spec-selective` flips 0012's selective adoption; it needs `--spec-adopt-selective`.
  - `predict-issue` stops or resumes speculative issue while the predictor and retention keep
    running; it needs `--predict-prefetch`.
  - `spec-gate` flips the configured 0013 gates to none; it needs a gate.
- Any other name is a config error, as is a lever without a toggle, the toggle with speculative
  decoding, and the toggle with `--route-ahead`.
- Every switch first drains speculation destructively (`ExpertStreamSource::reset_speculation()`), so
  no queued or half-read entry crosses from one arm into the other. The drain runs outside the timed
  decode and lands in the switch token's `loop_overhead_ms`.
- The per-token CSV gains four columns at the END: `spec_issued`, `spec_useful` (per-token deltas,
  also written without the toggle), `arm` (A/B, or `-`), `tokens_since_switch`. Existing columns are
  unchanged.
- The source's `Stats` gains `spec_issued`.
- A shutdown stderr line gives per-arm sums: tokens, wall, stall, compute, mgmt, flash MiB, spec
  issued/useful.

- builds (Release, `-j2`, capped). `bmoe_config_test` passes, including 11 new cases: the default
  changes nothing, valid levers accepted, unknown lever rejected, lever without toggle rejected,
  negative N rejected, each lever's prerequisite enforced, speculative decoding and non-streaming
  rejected. `bmoe_csv_summary_test` passes.
- **Lossless:** 5 runs of 96 tokens — no prefetch; `--predict-prefetch --predict-spec-max 3
  --spec-adopt-selective`; and that with each of the three levers toggled every 8 tokens. They
  generated byte-identical text (same md5 of the text before the `generation:` line).
- **The toggle does what it says** (per-arm stderr sums, 48 tokens per arm):
  - `spec-selective`: arm A turned 131 of 145 speculative reads into hits, arm B 14 of 143, the 0012
    mechanism seen inside one run.
  - `predict-issue`: arm B issued 0 speculative experts.
  - `spec-gate` (rank < 2): arm A issued 75 (all useful), arm B 143 (135 useful).
- `0014-DRAFT-ab_toggle_analyze.py` (the analysis gate, parked here rather than in `gates/`):
  - `--selftest` passes 5/5: identical arms are not resolved; a 10 ms effect is recovered (10.08)
    and resolved; a linear drift is cancelled by the sandwich pairing (0.05) while the adjacent
    pairing is biased (0.84); the carryover exclusion drops exactly W tokens per block; a
    non-toggled CSV is refused.
  - On the OLMoE `spec-selective` CSV (5 block pairs, laptop, slow external disk) it reports
    "not resolved" with the pairs needed, as a design this short should.

The laptop wall times are not meaningful (the model file is on a slow external disk). No rate is
claimed from these runs.

## Known wart

0012's shutdown line (`bmoe: spec-adopt-selective: ...`) prints twice per run, because `shutdown()`
is reached twice. It is cosmetic, but a parser that sums the line would double-count, so take the
first occurrence.
